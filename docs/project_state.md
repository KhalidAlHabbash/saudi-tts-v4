## Frozen Decision Gate A

- **Primary dataset:** SADA / Saudi Audio Dataset for Arabic (SDAIA + Saudi Broadcasting Authority), fully acquired from the public Hugging Face mirror `MohamedRashad/SADA22` at immutable revision `094fe2c0fe4b549a4f34349e6e0622e7c7273c2d`. All 31 Parquet/LFS hashes match that revision. Use only Saudi-labelled Najdi, Hijazi, and Khaliji material that passes the audio/transcript audit.
- **Dataset use constraint:** non-commercial research pilot under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Preserve source-level provenance and licence fields. Do not publish raw audio or trained weights without a separate rights review.
- **Supplemental datasets:** none for the prepared baseline. LDC2014S02, L2-KSU, SAAVB, SaudiTalk, and KSAA Aswat remain access/rights-gated candidates, not pipeline inputs.
- **Primary TTS model:** SILMA TTS v1, exact 162,666,852-parameter F5-TTS/DiT acoustic model, Hugging Face revision `ac81834c5ce305504fe4c7602187042fdd6913db`, paired with F5-TTS 1.1.7 source revision `c96c3aeed84f5e02aa54dc42c1193537ead39837` and fixed 13,531,650-parameter Vocos revision `0feb3fdd929bcd6649e0e7c5a688cf7dd012ef21`. All six cached asset hashes match `models/base/asset_manifest.json`.
- **Backup model:** OHF Piper (`piper1-gpl`) VITS, revision `404aefedbd74baa0bd43e451bc407a2b3aace0f5`, using an appropriately licensed medium warm-start checkpoint. The Arabic `ar_JO-kareem-medium` voice is a compatibility reference, not approved training material until checkpoint/data rights are verified.
- **Target sample rate:** 24,000 Hz, mono PCM WAV for processed audio. Preserve original SADA 16-kHz WAV files unchanged under `data/raw/`.
- **Canonical metadata:** UTF-8 CSV/JSONL with required `audio_path`, `text`, and `speaker_id`; retain `raw_text`, `dialect`, `region`, `gender`, `sample_rate`, `duration`, `dataset_source`, `source_id`, `split`, `license`, and `quality_flags`. The acquired mirror has no speaker identity, show, scene, or environment fields, so use an explicit unavailable sentinel rather than fabricating identities.
- **Split strategy (reopened after audit):** the selected mirror lacks every stable source/speaker grouping key assumed at Gate A. Preserve its official train/validation/test assignments; keep evaluation-priority rows (`test`, then `validation`, then `train`) and reject lower-priority cross-split collisions using both exact and punctuation/spacing/Arabic-diacritic-insensitive comparison keys. Seed all tie-breaking with `20260911`. Speaker leakage cannot be proven absent and must remain a reported limitation; do not silently claim speaker-disjointness.
- **Expected training approach:** full fine-tuning of the SILMA/F5 acoustic CFM/DiT from pinned EMA weights with fixed pretrained Vocos. The Mac profile is FP32, batch 1, accumulation 8, <=8-second clips, activation checkpointing, zero workers, and MPS-first. The RunPod Community RTX 4090 profile is single-GPU CUDA BF16, batch 2, accumulation 4, frames threshold 1,500, four workers, pinned memory, and TF32-enabled FP32 matmuls. Both retain an effective batch of eight and the same 120,000-update optimizer schedule; ordinary AdamW remains unfused and no GradScaler or `torch.compile` is used.
- **Python:** CPython 3.11.7 arm64 in the local `.venv`; Python 3.12 in a system-site-packages `.venv-runpod` on official template `runpod-torch-v280` / image `runpod/pytorch:1.0.2-cu1281-torch280-ubuntu2404`.
- **Dependency policy:** F5-TTS 1.1.7, PyTorch/TorchAudio 2.8.0, and the full transitive arm64 CPython 3.11 environment are version/hash locked in `requirements.lock.txt`; bootstrap consumes hashes and passes `pip check`. The PyPI F5 wheel SHA-256 is `8deb87c8f3968f6f4783e8ec76b255c9d87e29ec6c2e9b5f37b01732c8c80a6a`; PyPI metadata does not attest the configured upstream Git commit. Keep `attn_backend=torch`; do not install FlashAttention, bitsandbytes, Triton, TensorRT, DeepSpeed, or CUDA wheels.
- **Device strategy:** the local profile remains single-process MPS then CPU. The RunPod profile forbids non-CUDA training, uses standard BF16 autocast, and remains single-GPU/non-distributed. A full update-100 checkpoint was written and verified with model, EMA, optimizer, scheduler, progress, and RNG state. CUDA execution requires both production-shaped resume probes (batch1/acc8, then batch2/acc4) to finish two updates, update EMA, save/reload strictly, stay finite, and remain at or below 21.5 GiB CUDA peak reserved memory.
- **Known MPS limitations:** SILMA/F5 training is not vendor-validated end to end on MPS; begin in FP32 with `num_workers=0`; watch unsupported operations, allocator pressure, hangs, NaNs, and CPU/MPS divergence. Mixed precision is disabled until parity is demonstrated.
- **Major architecture decision:** the repository will wrap the pinned upstream F5/SILMA implementation rather than copy or silently fork large upstream code. Project-owned loaders, configs, launchers, evaluation, and narrowly scoped portability patches must remain auditable.
- **Measured raw data:** 253,166 clips / 437.545 h overall; 161,101 Najdi/Hijazi/Khaliji clips / 195.402 h before preprocessing. All payloads are 16-kHz mono PCM WAV; 0 missing/decode failures; 3 duplicate payloads. Exclude the `More than 1 speaker` label, unknown/non-Saudi dialects, missing/anomalous transcripts, and samples outside configured duration/quality limits.
- **Processed dataset:** after final Gate B remediation, 125,534 accepted clips / 148.722258 h and 127,632 rejected rows, materialized as 24-kHz mono PCM-16 WAV plus JSONL manifests. Split rows are 119,972 train / 2,717 validation / 2,845 test. Cross-split exact-audio, exact-text, and loose-text collision groups are all zero. - **Text policy:** preserve raw text separately; training text applies NFC and whitespace normalization only. Reject any hash-prefixed annotation/noise/uncertainty markup (4,104 rows), standalone uncertainty annotations, and underscore/hyphen machine-token forms at Arabic token boundaries. The final accepted manifest has zero `#` markers, zero standalone uncertainty annotations, and zero embedded machine-token forms. Use a separate loose key only for leakage detection, never as training text. No dialect-to-MSA conversion, ASR replacement, punctuation stripping, or diacritic destruction.
- **Model validation so far:** strict EMA load succeeds; an independent CPU one-step CFM update returned finite loss `0.56848729` and gradient norm `3.41564631`, then wrote and strictly restored model/optimizer/scheduler/EMA/progress/RNG state. The final Gate B dry-run finds 104,176 train and 2,330 validation rows under the configured 8-second limit with zero vocabulary OOV types.

Raw-audio, voice, or derived-weight publication and all commercial use are out of scope and remain blocked pending rightsholder/counsel and voice-consent review plus a complete attribution package.

Matching RunPod resumes preserve `next_batch`; mismatches fail before loading state. Only the known fingerprint-less update-100/epoch-0 Mac checkpoint may reset `next_batch` once during CUDA migration.
- **RunPod data arithmetic:** 104,176 eligible <=8-second train rows produce exactly 52,088 batch-two microbatches and 13,022 accumulation-four optimizer updates per epoch. Validation and test remain held out.
